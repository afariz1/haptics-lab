Intensity × sharpness

iOS Core Haptics and Android's envelope API describe a haptic with the same two values. Tap or drag on the field. Left = dull and low, right = crisp and high. Up = stronger.

Cheap motors ignore very short pulses. Tap from the left; the first one you feel tells you how short your patterns can be.
